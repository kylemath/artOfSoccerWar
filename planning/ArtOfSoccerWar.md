## ⚽ *The Art of War* for Soccer Coaching

**Chapters 1–4** | *For Youth Club Coaches and Players*
**Plain language, in book order**

---

### **Chapter 1 – Laying Plans**

1. **Plan to Win:** Good preparation matters more than just having strong players.
2. **Compare the Five:** Look at team spirit, coaching, game setting, plan, and discipline.
3. **Better Values Win:** The team with better leadership and values usually wins.
4. **Know Both Sides:** A coach must know their team *and* the other team to win.
5. **Use Deception:** Trick the other team — don't show what you're really doing.
6. **Hide Your Strength:** Act weak when you're strong. Act strong when you're weak.
7. **Opposite Moves:** If the other team wants to go fast, slow them down. If they want to rest, press them hard.
8. **Confuse Them:** Make them guess — change positions, mix up patterns.
9. **Test Them First:** Use small plays to learn how they respond.
10. **Don't Force It:** Only attack when it makes sense.
11. **Move As One:** A well-led team plays like one body.
12. **Stay Calm:** A calm coach leads better during a wild game.
13. **Force Errors:** Make the other team mess up by creating pressure.
14. **Know to Win:** If you know yourself and the other team, you'll win most games.

---

### **Chapter 2 – Waging War**

15. **Save Energy:** Don't use all your energy at the start.
16. **Use the Whole Team:** Don't depend on just a few players — rotate smartly.
17. **Avoid Long Battles:** Win fast when you can — long fights drain your team.
18. **Be Efficient:** Play sharp and don't waste moves.
19. **Momentum Helps:** A fast start can lift the whole team.
20. **Strike First:** Score early to shake their confidence.
21. **Pressure Early:** Make them uncomfortable at the start.
22. **Protect Team Spirit:** A tired or unhappy team doesn't play well.
23. **Think Before You Run:** Smart play beats nonstop running.
24. **Coach Smart:** Good coaches plan, teach, and lead — not just yell.
25. **Motivate Naturally:** Create a team that *wants* to work hard.
26. **Break Their Will:** Pressure can make the other team give up on their own.

---

### **Chapter 3 – Attack by Stratagem**

27. **Win Without Fighting:** The best win is when the other team gives up before a battle.
28. **Stop Their Plan:** Beat the other team by ruining their strategy early.
29. **Break Their Unity:** Confuse them or make them argue so they stop working as a team.
30. **Lower Their Confidence:** If you can't stop their teamwork, shake their belief in winning.
31. **Fight Last:** Try other ways before going into a full-out fight.
32. **Fast Wins Are Best:** Long fights cause problems. Win quickly when you can.
33. **Better Teams Can Lose:** Even a strong team can lose to a smarter one.
34. **Think Ahead:** The best coaches win by thinking more clearly than the other side.
35. **Protect First:** Make your team solid on defense before attacking.
36. **Don't Rush In:** Being brave without thinking leads to losing.
37. **Pick Your Way:** There are different ways to win — choose what works for your team.

---

### **Chapter 4 – Tactical Dispositions**

38. **Get Set First:** Make sure your team is fully ready before trying to win.
39. **Stay Organized:** A steady team stays calm when the game gets tough.
40. **Win with Setup:** Victory comes from good positioning and timing, not just effort.
41. **React Smartly:** Be ready to defend and respond before attacking.
42. **Avoid Bad Odds:** Don't fight unless the situation is good for you.
43. **Be Patient:** Wait until you see the right moment to strike.
44. **Don't Show Off:** Great plays should look simple — that means they were planned well.
45. **Just Win:** Good coaches don't care about praise — they focus on results.

---

### **Chapter 5 – Energy**

46. **Team Flow:** A great team flows like water — always moving, adjusting, and working together.
47. **Use Combined Power:** Good teams move as one, using passes and timing to create big impact.
48. **Mix Up Play:** Use both calm buildup and sudden attacks to confuse the opponent.
49. **Simple > Fancy:** Smart and simple plays beat wild and flashy ones.
50. **Control the Action:** Decide when to play fast or slow — don't let the other team control the pace.
51. **Lead the Rhythm:** Great teams change speeds to throw off the other team's timing.
52. **Sync Your Team:** When players move in sync, small actions create big results.
53. **Use Player Strengths:** Put each player where they do best — smart placement boosts the whole team.
54. **No Need for Chaos:** You don't need to play wild to win — control the field with simple, sharp play.

---

### **Chapter 6 – Weak Points and Strong**

55. **Attack Weak Spots:** Go after the other team's open spaces or slow players.
56. **Be Unpredictable:** Don't always attack the same way — keep them guessing.
57. **Move Like Water:** Flow around strong defenders and into the spaces they leave open.
58. **Show Then Strike:** Pretend to go one way, then change direction to find gaps.
59. **Move First:** Don't wait to react — force the other team to adjust to you.
60. **Create Pressure Gaps:** Pressure one side to open space on the other.
61. **Draw Them Out:** Make the other team chase — then strike when they lose shape.
62. **Hide Your Intentions:** Keep your real plan hidden until it's time to act.
63. **Avoid Their Strengths:** Don't go head-to-head with their best players — work around them.
64. **Stay Flexible:** Change your plan during the game if needed — adapt to how it's going.
65. **Speed Beats Size:** Fast and smart movements can beat bigger or stronger teams.

---

### **Chapter 7 – Maneuvering**

66. **Think Before Moving:** Don't rush forward — think carefully before changing your team's shape.
67. **Practice Transitions:** Moving the team from defense to attack (or back) needs practice to be smooth.
68. **Don't Get Stuck:** Avoid sending your team into tight spaces with no way out.
69. **Stay Close Together:** Spread out too far and you lose control; stay connected to support each other.
70. **Work As One:** If your team isn't working together, even great players won't win.
71. **Move With Purpose:** Don't waste running — move with intention and timing.
72. **Avoid Player Fatigue:** Be smart with energy so players aren't too tired to finish strong.
73. **Signal Clearly:** Make sure players understand signals — use simple hand signs or words.
74. **Talk Before the Game:** A quick word from the coach before the match can lift the whole team.
75. **Avoid Panic:** If a mistake happens, don't rush or panic — stay calm and reset.
76. **Know What Slows You:** Bad field conditions, tired players, or confusion hurt your team more than the opponent does.
77. **Keep Morale High:** A happy, confident team plays way better than a fearful one.

---

### **Chapter 8 – Variation in Tactics**

78. **Change It Up:** Don't use the same strategy every game — keep things fresh.
79. **Adjust to Conditions:** Change your game plan depending on the field, the ref, the weather, or the other team.
80. **Punish Mistakes:** If the other team messes up, strike quickly.
81. **Avoid Predictability:** If you always press or always defend the same way, good teams will figure it out.
82. **Don't Follow Just Habit:** Don't do something just because it worked once — every game is different.
83. **Stick to What Works:** But if a plan is working *right now*, don't switch it up just to be clever.
84. **Bad Coaching Hurts:** If a coach can't adapt, the team suffers.
85. **Handle Trouble Calmly:** When things go wrong, keep your cool — make a change, not a scene.
86. **React With Control:** When the other team surprises you, don't freeze — adjust quickly and with confidence.

### **Chapter 9 – The Army on the March**

87. **Watch Team Signs:** Pay attention to how your players act — their energy level tells you a lot.
88. **Read the Game:** Notice if the other team looks tired, confident, or worried.
89. **Stay Together:** Keep your team close enough to help each other quickly.
90. **Choose Good Ground:** Pick the best spots on the field for your style of play.
91. **Avoid Bad Positions:** Don't get trapped in corners or against the sideline.
92. **Stay Alert:** Watch for sudden changes in how the other team plays.
93. **Trust Your Eyes:** If something looks wrong, it probably is — trust what you see.
94. **Keep Formation:** Don't let your team get pulled apart and lose shape.
95. **Protect Each Other:** Make sure every player has teammate support nearby.
96. **Stay Balanced:** Don't send everyone forward — keep some players back for safety.

---

### **Chapter 10 – Terrain**

97. **Know Your Field:** Learn about every field you play on — size, surface, slopes.
98. **Use the Space:** Understand which parts of the field help your team play better.
99. **Check Conditions:** Look at sun position, wind, wet spots — they affect the game.
100. **Adapt to Surface:** Change your play style if needed for grass, turf, or hard ground.
101. **Find Advantages:** Use field features (like width or slope) to help your team.
102. **Control Key Areas:** Own the important spaces — like the center of the field.
103. **Safe Positions:** Keep players in spots where they can help each other.
104. **Smart Spacing:** Spread out enough to play, but stay close enough to help.
105. **Watch Both Ends:** Pay attention to both goals — attacks can come quickly.
106. **Home vs Away:** Adjust your strategy based on whether you're home or away.

---

### **Chapter 11 – Different Game Situations**

107. **Read the Game Flow:** Learn to spot different game situations and how to handle each one.
108. **Away Game Basics:** When playing away, stay extra focused and stick together as a team.
109. **Home Advantage:** On your home field, use your familiarity to play with confidence.
110. **Key Moments:** Some parts of the game matter more than others — know when to push hard.
111. **Team Connection:** Keep your players close enough to pass and help each other.
112. **Watch Both Teams:** Pay attention to how tired or confident both teams look.
113. **Protect the Lead:** When ahead, play smart and don't take unnecessary risks.
114. **Coming from Behind:** When behind, stay calm and look for good chances to score.
115. **Trust Building:** Let players know you believe in them — it builds their confidence.
116. **Clear Instructions:** Give simple, clear directions that everyone understands.
117. **Keep Secrets:** Don't tell everyone your game plan — surprise the other team.
118. **Stay Positive:** Even in tough spots, keep your team's spirit up.
119. **Build Team Trust:** When players trust each other, they play better together.
120. **Smart Subbing:** Make substitutions at the right time to help your team.
121. **Watch and Learn:** Study how other teams play to learn their strengths and weaknesses.
122. **Team Unity:** A united team plays better than talented players who don't work together.

---

### **Chapter 12 – Using Intensity**

123. **Energy Management:** Know when to have your team play with high or low intensity.
124. **Weather Watch:** Use the weather (sun, wind, rain) to your advantage when you can.
125. **Right Timing:** Pick the right moments to increase pressure on the other team.
126. **Smart Pressure:** Press hard when it makes sense, back off when it doesn't.
127. **Energy Bursts:** Use short bursts of high energy instead of constant pressure.
128. **Recovery Time:** Give players chances to catch their breath when possible.
129. **Match Tempo:** Control the game's speed — fast or slow — to help your team.
130. **Practice Intensity:** Train with different energy levels so players learn to adjust.
131. **Save Strength:** Don't waste energy early if you'll need it later.
132. **Team Spirit:** Keep your team's motivation high but controlled.
133. **Cool Heads:** Stay calm even when the game gets exciting.
134. **Smart Choices:** Only use high energy when there's a good chance it will help.

---

### **Chapter 13 – Game Intelligence**

135. **Watch and Learn:** Pay attention to how other teams warm up and play.
136. **Study Patterns:** Notice what other teams do in different situations.
137. **Share Knowledge:** Let players help each other by sharing what they see.
138. **Quick Reports:** Have assistant coaches watch different parts of the game.
139. **Learn Habits:** Notice other teams' favorite moves and plays.
140. **Keep Secrets:** Don't let other teams know your plans too early.
141. **Smart Questions:** Ask your players what they notice during the game.
142. **Team Watching:** Have players watch other games to learn new ideas.
143. **Trust Players:** Listen to your players when they tell you what's happening on the field.
144. **Quick Updates:** Share important information clearly and quickly.
145. **Learn Together:** After each game, talk about what worked and what didn't.
146. **Future Planning:** Use what you learn to make better plans for next time.
